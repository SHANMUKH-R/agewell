Preserve the shared persona/patient selection across demo views, and keep the walkthrough clearly labeled as synthetic rather than production clinical use.

**Why:** The investor demo needs coherent cross-persona storytelling without implying that synthetic scenarios, simulated notifications, or risk guidance are real clinical operations.

**How to apply:** Keep synthetic-data disclosure visible, preserve human-controlled emergency actions, and maintain one shared selected patient across Elder, Family, and Clinician views.
